{# Live trip dashboard: flight countdown, JST clock and an itinerary timeline with a "you are here" marker. Numbers, labels, status stamp and per-stop state are filled/animated client-side by initTrip() in app.js, read from the data-* attributes against the viewer's own clock. Each stop links into its region sub-album (cover + photo count), so the timeline doubles as navigation. The static markup is a sensible no-JS fallback. The matching route map (_trip_map.html) renders in the album sidebar — see album.html — and is state-synced by the same initTrip(). On colour: the module keeps its own three state colours (upcoming / here / done) plus the weather glyph's own tints, and spends them where they say something — the frame's top rule and the wash under it, the module's name, the clock panel's hairline, the status stamp, the leg dots, the day chip, the progress fill, the active leg's name, the map, and the sun/rain/snow glyph. What went grey is what was tinted for decoration rather than for meaning: the countdown DIGITS, the leg grounds, the labels, the hover.
